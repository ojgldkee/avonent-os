HOW THE BRAIN WORKS

From evidence to an ad worth testing.

Avonent combines verified product details with competitor observations to develop original creative concepts. It should never invent reviews, medical outcomes, sales performance, or unsupported product benefits.

1Understand

Use imported product facts and your optional positioning notes.

2Observe

Track selected competitor brands and save verified ad examples.

3Reason

Identify hooks, objections, offers and creative gaps.

4Create

Generate original hooks, scripts, headlines and test plans.

Live AI synthesis and scheduled competitor monitoring depend on backend services. This screen is a workspace overview, not a claim that those automations are already running.
