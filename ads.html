COMPETITOR PATTERNS

Use the structure, not the exact wording

These are patterns found in current Meta Ad Library research. Treat them as inspiration, not proof the ads are profitable.

ResiliaIngredient proof

Research-backed ingredient framing

Resilia is repeatedly using a headline around two heavily studied botanicals. The useful pattern is specificity + perceived research depth, not the exact headline.

Pattern: “Why these specific ingredients?” → proof/detail → product.

RosabellaTestimonial

Personal transformation story

Rosabella appears in testimonial-style creative that leads with a spouse/customer story and an emotionally clear outcome. Use the story structure without copying or making unsupported claims.

Pattern: person → frustration → discovery → believable change → CTA.

MetabolaeSpecificity

Precise ingredient + delivery detail

Metabolae is using very specific ingredient-equivalent and delivery-language headlines. The transferable lesson is concrete product detail instead of vague benefit language.

Pattern: exact product detail → why it matters → simple proof → offer.

ResiaProduct detail

Product fact as the headline

Resia is also running direct product-detail creative. The lesson is that a concrete formulation/delivery fact can be the hook when the audience already understands the category.

Pattern: concrete fact → mechanism/context → proof → risk reversal.
